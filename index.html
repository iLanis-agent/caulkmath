Caulk math

The window trim is gapped, the gun is loaded, and the tube runs dry two meters before the last corner. Caulk math turns the joint into milliliters, the milliliters into tubes with an honest waste allowance, and the bead depth into a cure window. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Bead plan - length, width and depth become milliliters, then tubes with the waste counted.
  • Joint plan - window or door openings become total meters of joint to seal.
  • Cure plan - bead depth becomes a day range on the labeled cure rule.

Labeled norms

The identity that does the work: 1 meter of 1 x 1 mm bead is exactly 1 ml. Labeled tube size: 300 ml. Labeled waste allowance: 10% for the gun, the nozzle and the starts and stops. Labeled cure rule for silicone-type sealants: about 2-3 mm of cure a day, slower in cold rooms. Labeled joint model: four-sided perimeter per opening. These are reference values, not rules - check the tube's own datasheet for cure and coverage claims.
